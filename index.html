ColorLand

Find the parts, colour them, learn their names — for kids 3-8

Can you find the wing? ColorLand asks for a part of the picture by name, your child finds it and taps it, colours it in, and learns what it is called — in nine languages. Then the whole picture is theirs to colour.

No ads. No accounts. No tracking. No in-app purchases. ColorLand is a paid download and everything is unlocked from the first launch. Everything a child colours stays on the device, and sharing, saving and printing sit behind a grown-ups gate.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

ColorLand is a colouring-book app for children. We designed it so that we collect no personal information at all — not from children, and not from grown-ups.

  • No accounts. The app never asks for a name, email or any login. "Profiles" inside the app are just a nickname and an emoji stored on the device so siblings can keep separate galleries.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. ColorLand contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. We receive nothing about you or your child.
  • Pictures stay on your device. Coloured pictures and drawings are saved only on the device.
  • The app never uses the internet. ColorLand is a paid download with nothing to buy inside it, so it makes no network requests at all. There is no advertising, no analytics and no server of ours for it to talk to.
  • Sharing, saving and printing. These use your device's own share sheet, photo library and print dialog. The picture goes wherever you choose to send it — we are not involved. All three are behind a grown-ups gate (a simple multiplication question).
  • Device backup. Like other apps, whatever ColorLand stores on the device may be included in your own device backup (iCloud or Google), which is controlled by you and by your device's operating system, not by us. You can also export a backup file yourself from Grown-ups → Backup.
  • Notifications. The optional weekly reminder is scheduled locally on the device. No server is involved and no push service receives your data.
  • No third parties. We do not sell, transfer or disclose any information to third parties, because we do not have any.
  • Children's privacy. ColorLand is designed to comply with COPPA and GDPR-K: it collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

If you have questions about this policy, email youtubeabdulla@gmail.com.
